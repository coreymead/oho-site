A layout assistant for the room you're standing in

Look at a wall and Oho draws the marks you need, full size, right where they go.

Level lines, drill points, measurements and estimated stud positions, on your real wall. Plan a row of frames, shelves, a TV mount, a handrail, picture-frame molding or wainscot before you drill, cut or paint.

Free: the hand tools

No account, no internet, no limits.

  • Level line. Pinch a wall and a level line runs around the whole room.
  • Measure. Pinch two points to get the distance, straight across or straight up.
  • Drill point. Mark the exact spot, with the two numbers a tape measure needs to find it again.
  • Depth. Push the marks in or out until they sit right on your real wall, so a pencil mark lands where the drawing says.
  • Guided tour. A short tour on the board in front of you teaches each tool, one step at a time.

Things you can ask

Hanging and marking
  • "Hang my 24 by 36 inch picture centered on this wall, with the middle 57 inches off the floor."
  • "Mount my TV centered on this wall with the middle of the screen 42 inches up, and show me where the studs probably are."
  • "Make a gallery grid: two rows of three 12 inch frames, 4 inches apart, centered on the wall."
  • "Mark the bracket screws for a curtain rod over this window."
  • "Put a handrail on this stair wall, 36 inches above the steps."
Molding and cabinets
  • "Lay out picture-frame molding on this wall: three boxes, 30 wide and 48 tall. How many feet of molding do I need?"
  • "Run a chair rail at 36 inches around the whole room. How much molding is that?"
  • "How many feet of baseboard do I need? No baseboard across the door."
  • "Lay out base cabinets along this wall."
Paint, flooring and tile estimates
  • "How much paint do I need for all four walls? Two coats."
  • "I'm tiling the floor with 12 inch square tiles. How many tiles do I need?"
  • "Flooring is $3.50 a square foot. What will that cost for this room?"
Will it fit?
  • "Will a 90 inch sofa fit against this wall?"

Good to know

A guide, not a guarantee. Marks come from your headset's room scan, which is not exact, and walls are rarely perfectly flat. Check that the marks line up with real corners and edges from two angles, mark the spot lightly in pencil, then check your measurements with a tape measure before you drill, cut or buy.
  • Material amounts are estimates. Measure the wall yourself and confirm quantities with your supplier before you buy.
  • Stud marks are estimates, not detections. Confirm them with a stud finder or a stud magnet, and never drill directly above or below an outlet or switch.
  • Needs a room map from your headset's Space Setup.
  • Ask Oho needs a microphone and an internet connection. What you say is turned into text by Meta's Wit.ai speech service. The text and a simplified model of your room (wall sizes, windows, doors) are sent to our server and an AI model provider to work out the layout. No camera images or video are sent, and our server does not keep your voice, words or room data after it answers. See the privacy policy.
