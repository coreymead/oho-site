Getting started

The first time you open Oho it runs a short tour on the board in front of you. You can run it again from the Tour button.

Common questions

Oho says it can't find my room. Tap Capture room on the board and finish the headset's room scan. Oho needs the map to know where your walls are.

The marks look slightly off the wall. Tap Depth, aim at the wall and push the marks in or out until they sit on it. That makes a pencil mark land where the drawing says.

Ask Oho doesn't hear me. Hold the pinch while you talk and let go to send. Allow the microphone when the headset asks, and check your internet connection.

How accurate are the marks and measurements? Oho works from your headset's map of the room, which is not exact, so treat every mark as a guide. Check that the marks line up with real corners and edges from two angles, mark the spot lightly in pencil, then check your measurements with a tape measure before you drill, cut or buy. Amounts such as square footage or paint come from the same map, so check the room's main measurements with a tape before you order.

How can I get a better room scan? Clear the room, turn the lights on, and walk the walls slowly in Space Setup. If a wall looks off, tap Rescan room and do it again.

Are the stud marks accurate? No. They are estimates. Check them with a stud finder or a stud magnet before drilling, and never drill directly above or below an outlet or switch.

Where do I manage a purchase? Purchases go through Meta. See your Meta account or the Meta Horizon Store for receipts and refunds.

Safety

Oho is a layout aid, not a substitute for judgment. Wiring, plumbing and structure can be hidden behind a wall. When in doubt, ask a professional.
