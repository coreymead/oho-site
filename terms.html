What Oho is

Oho helps you plan the position of marks and fixtures on real surfaces. It is a planning aid. It does not detect studs, wiring or pipes, and it does not check building codes.

Your responsibility

Before you drill, cut or buy: look at whether the marks line up with real corners and edges from two angles, mark the spot lightly in pencil, and check your measurements with a tape measure. You are responsible for checking every measurement with a tape measure and every stud, wire and pipe location with the proper tools before you drill, cut or fasten anything. Rules for heights, spacing and safety vary by place; where Oho names a common convention, it is not a legal requirement, so check your local code.

Estimates, not guarantees

Oho relies on your headset's map of the room and on automated suggestions. Both can be wrong. We do not guarantee the accuracy of any mark, measurement, estimate or suggestion.
