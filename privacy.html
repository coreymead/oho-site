Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Oho is made by Corey Mead ("we"). This explains what Oho handles, why, and who else sees it.

What Oho handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your voice, only while you hold the pinch in Ask OhoTo turn what you say into wordsSent from your headset to Meta's Wit.ai speech service, which turns it into text. We do not receive your audio
What you said, as textTo work out the layout you asked forOur server, then an AI model provider (Anthropic's Claude) to plan the layout
Your room's dimensions and features (wall sizes, windows, doors) and the marks currently drawnSo the layout fits your real wallsSent with each request to our server and the AI model provider. No camera images or video are sent.
The reply, as text and speechTo tell you what Oho drewOur server, and a speech service (Meta's Wit.ai) that turns the reply into spoken audio
Your marks and settingsTo keep your work between sessionsStored on your headset only
Your Meta user ID, purchases, and how many requests you have leftTo check your pass, count your requests and verify purchasesMeta, and our server. We also keep a usage log (time of each request and whether it worked, not what was said) for 90 days.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your voice or room data for advertising. Oho does not use the headset's cameras. By design, our server does not keep your voice, your words or your room data after it answers; it keeps only what it needs to count requests against a pass. Technical logs record timing and errors, not what you said.

Voice and your choices

Oho asks for microphone permission, and only records while you hold the pinch. You can use all the hand tools with no voice and no account. If you do not want your voice processed by a speech service, do not use Ask Oho.

Other companies that handle data for us

  • Meta (the Meta Horizon Store, in-app purchases, and Wit.ai for speech). Meta's own privacy terms apply.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI model that plans layouts.
  • Amazon Web Services, where our server runs.

Keeping and deleting data

Marks and settings are on your headset and are removed when you clear them in the app or uninstall it. For anything we hold about you (such as a pass record), write to mead.corey@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Children

Oho is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact
