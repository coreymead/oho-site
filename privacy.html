Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. Oho is made by Corey Mead ("we"). This explains what Oho handles, why, and who else sees it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your voice or room data for advertising. Oho does not use the headset's cameras. By design, our server does not keep your voice, your words or your room data after it answers; it keeps only what it needs to count requests against a pass. Technical logs record timing and errors, not what you said.

Voice and your choices

Oho asks for microphone permission, and only records while you hold the pinch. You can use all the hand tools with no voice and no account. If you do not want your voice processed by a speech service, do not use Ask Oho.

Other companies that handle data for us

  • Meta (the Meta Horizon Store, in-app purchases, and Wit.ai for speech). Meta's own privacy terms apply.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI model that plans layouts, and Wit.ai, which turns speech into text. They receive only what you say, the room description and the current marks, never your Meta user ID. We do not control how long they keep that; their own terms apply.
  • Fly.io, which runs our server, and Amazon Web Services, where backups of our database are stored. They handle your Meta user ID only to host the server and its backups.

Deleting your data

Marks and settings are on your headset and are removed when you clear them in the app or uninstall it.

To delete what we hold about you on our server (your Meta user ID, request counts, pass records and usage log), email mead.corey@gmail.com with the subject "Oho data deletion". Deleting your data is free. Include the support code shown at the bottom of Oho's menu, which lets us find your account. We will confirm by email and delete it within 30 days, and usually within a week. Our database backups are overwritten within about 3 days after that.

We cannot delete records that Meta keeps, such as your purchase history and your Meta account. Ask Meta for those. We may decline or delay a request that we cannot verify, that is repeated or abusive, or for records we are required to keep, and if so we will tell you why.

Children

Oho is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
